Albay Park & Wildlife

A green heart in the middle of Legazpi City. Connect with nature, discover local wildlife, and enjoy a peaceful day of learning and relaxation with the whole family.


ABOUT THE PARK

Albay Park and Wildlife is a sprawling nature reserve that serves as a home to various endemic animals and lush tropical plants. It is a premier destination for families and students, offering a perfect blend of environmental education and outdoor recreation.

CONSERVATION HISTORY

The park was developed with the mission to promote environmental awareness and wildlife conservation in the Bicol region. Over the years, it has transformed from a simple conservation area into a popular educational hub, teaching visitors the importance of protecting our natural heritage.

FACTS

Key information about the park's facilities.

  • Location: Legazpi City, Albay
  • Animals: Various Local Species
  • Amenities: Picnic Areas & Lagoon
  • Focus: Wildlife Conservation

TRAVEL TIPS

Essentials for a comfortable outdoor visit.

  • 👟 Footwear: Wear comfortable walking shoes.
  • 💧 Hydration: Bring water and snacks for a picnic.
  • ⏰ Timing: Morning visits are cooler and better for animals.
  • 🚫 Safety: Strictly do not feed the animals.
