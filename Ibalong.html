Ibalong Festival

Witness the legends of Bicol come to life. Celebrate the epic tales of ancient heroes through vibrant street dances, ornate costumes, and the rhythmic heartbeat of Legazpi City.


ABOUT THE FESTIVAL

The Ibalong Festival is a non-religious celebration held in Legazpi City. It is a colorful spectacle of street theater and dance that showcases the resilience and rich culture of Albay. Visitors are treated to a lively atmosphere filled with music, sports events, and trade fairs.

AN EPIC LEGACY

The festival is inspired by the "Ibalong Epic," a fragment of a 60-stanza narrative poem. It commemorates the legendary heroes Baltog, Handiong, and Bantong, who fought to protect the land from mythical monsters. This yearly event honors the bravery and ancient heritage of the Bicolano people.

FACTS

Festival schedule and origins.

  • Date: Every Month of August
  • Location: Legazpi City, Albay
  • Inspiration: The Ibalong Epic
  • Type: Cultural & Secular

TRAVEL TIPS

Enjoy the parade comfortably.

  • 👕 Attire: Wear light, breathable cotton clothing.
  • ☀️ Protection: Use sunscreen and bring a hat or umbrella.
  • 💧 Hydration: Carry water, as the parades last for hours.
  • 📸 Photography: Arrive at the viewing deck early for photos.
