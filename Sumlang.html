Sumlang Lake

A serene sanctuary in Camalig, Sumlang Lake offers the most iconic reflected view of Mayon Volcano. Experience the tranquility of bamboo rafting while surrounded by the natural artistry of Albay.


<<<<<<< HEAD

ABOUT THE DESTINATION

Sumlang Lake is a peaceful spot with a clear view of Mayon Volcano. It is perfect for relaxing and enjoying nature. Visitors can try bamboo rafting and take stunning photos with the volcano's reflection on the water.

HISTORY & DEVELOPMENT

The lake was developed as a tourist attraction by the local community of Camalig. Over time, it became popular for its scenic beauty and cultural features. It continues to attract both local and foreign visitors seeking peace.

FACTS

Key information about this lakeside destination.

  • Location: Camalig, Albay
  • Feature: Mirror-view of Mayon
  • Activity: Bamboo Rafting
  • Vibe: Peaceful & Natural

TRAVEL TIPS

How to make the most of your lakeside visit.

  • 🛶 Wear: Comfortable clothes for kayaking.
  • 📸 Camera: Bring one for the scenic mirror views.
  • ⏰ Timing: Arrive early to avoid the tourist crowds.
  • 🌿 Respect: Keep the local environment clean.

THINGS TO DO

Popular activities for families and solo travelers.

Bamboo Rafting Kayaking Picnic Sightseeing Walking Paths
