Donsol Whale Sharks

Swim with the "Gentle Giants" of the ocean. Experience a world-class, ethical interaction with whale sharks in their natural habitat.


<<<<<<< HEAD

ABOUT THE EXPERIENCE

Donsol is internationally recognized as the "Whale Shark Capital of the World." Unlike other destinations, Donsol focuses on natural interactions where visitors can snorkel alongside these massive, peaceful creatures.

ECO-TOURISM HISTORY

Tourism in Donsol began in 1998 after the local community realized the value of protecting the whale sharks. Today, it serves as a global model for sustainable marine tourism.

FACTS

  • Peak Season: February to May
  • Sightings: November to June
  • Local Name: "Butanding"
  • Practice: Eco-Friendly

TRAVEL TIPS

  • 🚫 No Touching: Maintain 3-meter distance.
  • 🧴 Sunscreen: Use reef-safe lotions.
  • 📵 No Flash: Photography must be natural.
  • 🏊 Gear: Snorkel and mask required.
