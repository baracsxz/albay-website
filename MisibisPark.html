Misibis Adventure Park

Fuel your adrenaline on Cagraray Island. From rugged ATV trails to soaring ziplines, this is where world-class luxury meets heart-pounding outdoor adventure.


ABOUT THE ADVENTURE PARK

Misibis Adventure Park offers a wide array of exciting outdoor activities designed for thrill-seekers. Whether you're navigating the volcanic terrain on an ATV or flying across the canopy on a zipline, the park provides a safe yet exhilarating environment for visitors of all ages.

ORIGIN & GROWTH

Originally developed as an extension of the luxury Misibis Bay resort, the Adventure Park was created to transform the island into a full-service tourism destination. Today, it stands as a separate highlight, known for its unique trails and professional-grade outdoor facilities.

FACTS

Essentials for your island adventure.

  • Activities: ATV, Zipline, Dune Buggy
  • Location: Near Misibis Bay Resort
  • Accessibility: Island-based Adventure
  • Target: Family & Group Friendly

TRAVEL TIPS

How to prepare for the thrills.

  • 👕 Outfit: Wear activewear and closed shoes.
  • 🧤 Safety: Always follow the guide’s instructions.
  • 🎟️ Booking: Recommended to book activities early.
  • 🎒 Extras: Bring extra clothes for after the trails.
